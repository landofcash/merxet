Merxet testnet ordering privacy notice

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This notice covers the Merxet AI-assisted ordering MVP pilot on Hedera testnet. Mainnet ordering is not available through this integration.

Information used for an order

When you ask a connected AI application to prepare an order, the local Merxet integration processes:

  • The catalogue seed, selected product identifiers, and quantities.
  • Delivery information you provide, such as your name, delivery address, contact details, and delivery comments.
  • Quote metadata, testnet payment requirements, approval status, and public testnet order evidence.

Delivery-information protection

The local integration encrypts delivery information before retaining the pending intent. The Merxet testnet quote service processes the delivery information transiently to validate the request and construct the quote. Its quote record retains the encrypted delivery envelope, not the plaintext delivery information or delivery key.

If you approve and complete the order, the encrypted delivery information is associated with the testnet order so the selected seller can fulfil it. Do not use real delivery information unless you intend to share it with that seller for the pilot.

Local and testnet records

Pending intent files are stored on your computer. On Windows, the default location is %LOCALAPPDATA%\Merxet\mcp. These files contain encrypted delivery information and quote or status metadata; they do not retain plaintext delivery information or the delivery key. They remain until you remove the local Merxet MCP data.

Server-side testnet quote data is retained only through its configured quote and recovery period. Hedera testnet transactions and associated public order evidence are public ledger records and cannot be deleted by Merxet.

Wallet boundary

The Merxet MCP integration cannot read private keys or mnemonics, unlock a wallet, sign a transaction, or approve payment. It returns an official Merxet browser link. You decide whether to connect a wallet and approve the testnet transaction there.

Contact

Questions or pilot privacy requests can be submitted through the Merxet GitHub issue tracker. Do not include delivery information, wallet details, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
